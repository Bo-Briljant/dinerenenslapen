Dine and stay in Overijssel

Where we dine and sleep in Overijssel

From the canals of Zwolle to the estates of Twente and the waterways of Giethoorn. Overijssel has a remarkable number of starred restaurants with rooms of their own.

In Overijssel the book holds four addresses for now. When we come across more that belong here, they go in. If we had to pick one, it would be De Lindenhof. If you are coming for a star, try De Lindenhof, De Bloemenbeek, De Swarte Ruijter and De Librije & Librije's Hotel. The next place is still empty: that is the address we are trying next.

Questions about dining and staying in Overijssel

Where can you dine and stay the night in Overijssel?

Our book lists four addresses in Overijssel: De Lindenhof in Giethoorn, De Bloemenbeek in De Lutte, De Swarte Ruijter in Holten and De Librije & Librije's Hotel in Zwolle.

Where would you go yourselves in Overijssel?

De Lindenhof in Giethoorn. A thatched farmhouse in Giethoorn where Martin Kruithof has been cooking at the top level since the nineties. What draws us is that steady hand, and a night with the kitchen garden next door. If it is full, we ring De Bloemenbeek.

What does dinner with an overnight stay cost in Overijssel?

For two, count on € 340 at De Swarte Ruijter up to € 1,430 at De Librije & Librije's Hotel, for dinner, one night and breakfast. Amounts come from their own booking systems, for a weeknight in November 2026.

Can you stay the night at a Michelin-starred restaurant in Overijssel?

Yes: at De Lindenhof in Giethoorn, De Bloemenbeek in De Lutte, De Swarte Ruijter in Holten and De Librije & Librije's Hotel in Zwolle. After dessert you just walk upstairs.
