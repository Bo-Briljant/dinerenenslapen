Our verdict

Dining and staying at Inter Scaldes

Inter Scaldes in Kruiningen is a name with weight. The restaurant was founded in 1968 in a manor in the Zeeland polder, between the Oosterschelde and the Westerschelde, and for decades it was one of the best-known addresses in the country. Since the reopening in 2024 Jeroen Achtien has been cooking here, and within a year he won two Michelin stars at once. Of all the kitchens in our book, this is one of the very strongest.

Achtien has Frisian roots, cooked for years in Switzerland and earned his first stars at Sens in Vitznau. In Kruiningen he combines that with top produce from Zeeland. His cooking is creative, technically strong and exuberant, with fermentation as a standard tool. He serves not courses but impressions of flavour, in a menu that follows the tides. Sanne Achtien welcomes the guests. The manor was thoroughly renovated and now feels luxurious and contemporary, with a bright extension facing the garden.

So why is it third in Zeeland? Because of the price. An evening with the big menu and a night in a suite costs considerably more here than at any other place in our book, and that weighs in our scores. Those who come for the kitchen get something truly exceptional in return. The twelve suites are next to the restaurant and belong to Pillows Hotels. You can only book them together with a table: reserving a room on its own is not possible.

Dining at Inter Scaldes: Jeroen Achtien's Tides Menu

The menu at Inter Scaldes is called the Tides Menu, and the name is more than a clever idea. As the sea moves, the menu moves with the season, the produce and time. Instead of courses the kitchen serves impressions of flavour, and you choose the tide that suits you: Low Tide, High Tide with twelve impressions or Spring Tide with eighteen. There are wine pairings, and there is also an à la carte menu that includes middle courses with caviar.

In high cuisine Jeroen Achtien looks for balance and harmony, with fermentation as an extra challenge for bringing ingredients together in new, daring forms. Michelin praises, for example, a dish in which he uses different parts of the cod, from cheeks and tongue to fillet, with nori, cabbage and an emulsion of fermented red cabbage. It is creative, sometimes complex and always grounded in craftsmanship.

The evening begins with snacks in the lounge, after which you walk past the kitchen for an extra bite and a look at the brigade. Details like that turn a two-star evening into one that stays with you. Our verdict: this is cooking at the highest level the Netherlands has to offer, with a voice of its own. The restaurant is open for dinner Thursday to Sunday and for lunch Friday to Sunday.

Staying at Inter Scaldes: the suites of Pillows Luxury Boutique Hotel

You sleep at Pillows Luxury Boutique Hotel Inter Scaldes, next to the restaurant. There are twelve suites in four types: Junior Suites, Junior Suites with terrace, Split-level Suites with terrace and the Inter Scaldes Suite with balcony. Every one has a king-size bed with Fables & Feathers linen.

On arrival you get an aperitif on the terrace, after dinner you walk straight to your suite, and the next morning a gastronomic breakfast from the restaurant's own kitchen awaits. As far as we are concerned, that breakfast is no afterthought: after an evening of eighteen impressions you want the morning to carry on at the same level, and that is the idea here.

Our pick is a suite with a terrace, so you can sit outside in the morning looking out over the polder. Note one rule: the suites can only be booked together with a table in the restaurant. If you only want to stay the night, this is not the place. There are packages for one or two nights, with the High Tide or Spring Tide menu, and one that includes a tour and tasting at De Oesterij in Yerseke.

The building: a manor from 1927 and an institution since 1968

Inter Scaldes is Latin for between the Scheldt rivers, and that is exactly where it lies: in the wide polders of South Beveland, between the Oosterschelde and the Westerschelde. The restaurant was founded in 1968 by Maartje and Kees Boudeling, in a manor from 1927, and grew into one of the best-known gastronomic addresses in the Netherlands.

In 2023 Jeroen and Sanne Achtien arrived from Switzerland, and the house underwent a thorough transformation. The old manor is now lounge-like and luxurious, with curving seats that create intimacy and a bright extension overlooking the garden. The whole, restaurant and hotel, is part of Pillows Hotels, a collection of boutique hotels in the Netherlands and Belgium. Inter Scaldes' former sous-chef, Paul de Groote, now cooks at LOF Restaurant at Pillows Hotel Reylof in Ghent.

Location and surroundings: things to do around Kruiningen and Yerseke

Inter Scaldes is on the Zandweg in Kruiningen, on South Beveland. If you want to stretch an evening here into a weekend, there is plenty of choice. In Yerseke, a few kilometres away, is De Oesterij, with historic oyster pits on the Oosterschelde, tours and tastings. You can even taste the chef's oyster there, a preparation Jeroen Achtien created for that initiative.

Along the Oosterschelde you walk and cycle through a landscape that changes with the tide, and from Wemeldinge or Zierikzee you can take a boat onto the water. For culture, head to Middelburg, with the Zeeuws Museum in the old abbey, to Veere, or to Domburg, where Mondrian and Toorop painted the Zeeland light. Antwerp is about forty minutes by car, which makes Inter Scaldes a natural choice for Belgian guests too.

By car you park free at the restaurant. By train, get off at Kruiningen-Yerseke station, on the line between Roosendaal and Vlissingen; from there it is a short taxi ride.

Who is Inter Scaldes for?

Inter Scaldes is for those who put the kitchen above everything else and are happy to pay exceptionally for an exceptional evening. It is the place for a big anniversary, a birthday you will never forget, or simply for the food lover who wants to know why Achtien won two stars so quickly. With a suite next door, you need not go anywhere after eighteen impressions and their wines.

If price matters, you will get more evening for your money elsewhere in Zeeland. Those who only want a room or want to go on a whim are not in the right place either: the suites come with a table, the restaurant is closed Monday to Wednesday and there are closing weeks. So plan ahead.

Good to know

The suites can only be booked together with a table, and the restaurant is closed Monday to Wednesday.

Frequently asked questions about Inter Scaldes

How many Michelin stars does Inter Scaldes have?

Inter Scaldes in Kruiningen holds two Michelin stars. Chef Jeroen Achtien won them within a year of the renewed restaurant reopening in 2024. Gault&Millau gives Inter Scaldes 17 points.

Can you stay at Inter Scaldes without dining?

No. The suites at Pillows Luxury Boutique Hotel Inter Scaldes can only be booked together with a restaurant reservation. You can also book a package with the High Tide or Spring Tide menu, a night and breakfast.

What is the Tides Menu at Inter Scaldes?

The Tides Menu at Inter Scaldes consists not of courses but of impressions of flavour. You choose Low Tide, High Tide with twelve impressions or Spring Tide with eighteen. The menu follows the season and the produce and is created by chef Jeroen Achtien and his team.

What does dinner with an overnight stay at Inter Scaldes cost?

For two, we count on about 895 euros for dinner, one night and breakfast at Inter Scaldes in Kruiningen (midweek, November 2026). That is the Spring Tide package with eighteen impressions and a Junior Suite. Wine comes on top.

When is Inter Scaldes open?

Inter Scaldes is open for dinner Thursday to Sunday from 18.30 and for lunch Friday to Sunday from 12.00. A few times a year the restaurant closes for a week or more; the dates are on its own website.

How do you get to Inter Scaldes in Kruiningen?

Inter Scaldes is at Zandweg 2 in Kruiningen, with free parking at the restaurant. By train, travel to Kruiningen-Yerseke station and take a taxi for the last stretch. From Antwerp it is about forty minutes by car.
