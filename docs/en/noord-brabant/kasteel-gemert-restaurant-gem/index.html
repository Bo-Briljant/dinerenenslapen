Our verdict

Dining and staying at Kasteel Gemert & Restaurant GEM.

Restaurant GEM. at Kasteel Gemert is, for us, the best place in North Brabant to dine and stay the night. Soenil Bahadoer, who previously earned two stars at De Lindehof in Nuenen, opened a new restaurant in July 2025 in the medieval gatehouse of a castle dating from 1391. Three months later he again held two Michelin stars, and Gault&Millau and the Lekker list placed him straight among the country's best. We rarely see a start like that.

What draws us so much is that his cooking is entirely his own. Bahadoer combines the spices and traditions of his Surinamese-Hindustani background with French precision and Dutch seasonal produce. It is not fusion for the sake of it, but a handwriting you will taste nowhere else: king crab with mango and green curry, duck with hoisin and moksi alesi, and a rendang as signature dish. There is a six-course menu and a fully plant-based version, and both are taken equally seriously.

For now you can stay in two suites: the Donjon Tower Suite in the castle itself, with centuries-old beams, and a Garden Suite in the castle grounds, looking out at the castle. In 2027 a full five-star hotel with seventy rooms and a spa opens here, but we see that as a reason to go now, while it is still small and intimate. The reservation: with two suites you are quickly too late, and an evening with an overnight stay is among the pricier in our book. The Dine & Stay package only applies on Wednesdays and Thursdays.

Dining at Restaurant GEM.: Soenil Bahadoer and the flavours of Suriname

GEM. is the realisation of Soenil Bahadoer's lifelong dream. It is explicitly not a continuation of De Lindehof but a new chapter, built on his own roots. The empty medieval gatehouse of Kasteel Gemert was transformed into an intimate fine-dining restaurant, warm and elegant. You reach it through the castle's kitchen garden.

The kitchen layers Surinamese-Hindustani spices, French precision and Dutch seasonal produce in dishes that feel both familiar and daring. The six-course GEM. menu opens with king crab with mango, radish and green curry, and continues with turbot with carrot, tandoori and coconut, scallop with pork belly and miso, duck with beetroot, hoisin and moksi alesi, and foie gras with plum and rum. Dessert is pandan with coconut, galangal and jasmine. The plant-based menu is just as outspoken, including saag paneer with samosa and potato masala. As an extra you can order the rendang, one of Bahadoer's signature dishes.

The menu comes with a classic wine pairing, a prestige pairing with rare wines and an alcohol-free pairing. Our verdict: this is one of the most exciting kitchens in the Netherlands, from a chef who knows exactly who he is. GEM. is open Wednesday to Sunday.

Staying at Kasteel Gemert: the Donjon Tower Suite and the Garden Suite

For now, staying at GEM. is a small-scale affair. The Donjon Tower Suite is inside the fourteenth-century castle itself, where centuries-old beams meet modern calm. The Garden Suite is in the castle grounds, looking out at the castle. In the morning the GEM. team prepares breakfast, and afterwards you step straight into the castle garden.

On Wednesday and Thursday evenings there is the Dine & Stay package: Soenil Bahadoer's six-course menu, a night on the castle grounds, champagne in your room or the minibar if you prefer, and breakfast the next morning. Drinks with dinner are not included. As far as we are concerned, the Donjon Tower Suite is the one to go for: sleeping in the tower of a castle from 1391 is not something you do often.

In 2027 Kasteel Gemert opens as an independent five-star hotel, with seventy rooms and suites in the castle and the Jesuit Wing, three restaurants and bars including a whisky bar, and a spa beneath stone vaults with an infinity pool that flows into the moat and three saunas. Until then you have the castle almost to yourself, which is exactly the charm of now.

The building: a castle from 1391 with a long history

Kasteel Gemert dates from 1391 and was built for the Teutonic Order, the order of knights that kept a commandery here for centuries. The castle has since lived many lives: among other things it was a monastery, a mill and a missionary school, and one wing still bears the name of the Jesuits who lived there. A green-lacquered gate leads onto the grounds, with a moat, a kitchen garden and a towering giant sequoia.

The restaurant is in the medieval gatehouse, which stood empty before Bahadoer made it his dream. The castle itself is being converted into a luxury hotel where contemporary design and heritage meet, with vegetables from the castle's own land on the table. Being able to sleep in the tower now, while the rest of the castle prepares for a new chapter, makes a visit at this moment all the more special.

Location and surroundings: things to do around Gemert and the Peel

Kasteel Gemert is on the Kasteellaan in the centre of Gemert, in the Brabant Peel, east of Eindhoven. It is a traditional Brabant village with a lively centre, and the castle grounds themselves invite a stroll along the moat and through the gardens.

In the village is the Boerenbondsmuseum, which shows what farming life in this region looked like around 1900. For nature, drive to De Groote Peel National Park, one of the last raised bogs in the Netherlands, with boardwalk trails and plenty of birds. There are also woods and heathland around Gemert for cycling, and Helmond Castle is a short drive away. Eindhoven, with the Van Abbemuseum and Strijp-S, is about half an hour by car.

By car Gemert is easy to reach from Eindhoven and Helmond. By public transport, take the train to Helmond or Eindhoven and then a bus or taxi to Gemert.

Who is Restaurant GEM. at Kasteel Gemert for?

GEM. is for those looking for cooking they will not find anywhere else, and for anyone who has always wanted to experience Soenil Bahadoer. It suits lovers of spice and bold flavours, plant-based eaters who deserve a proper starred menu, and couples who want a special night in a castle tower.

It suits those expecting a complete hotel with a spa and facilities less well: that only arrives in 2027. Spontaneous visitors have little chance with two suites, and if price matters, an exceptional evening here also comes at an exceptional price. So plan ahead and choose a Wednesday or Thursday.

Good to know

There are only two suites so far and Dine & Stay is only available on Wednesdays and Thursdays; the full castle hotel opens in 2027.

Frequently asked questions about Kasteel Gemert & Restaurant GEM.

How many Michelin stars does Restaurant GEM. have?

Restaurant GEM. at Kasteel Gemert holds two Michelin stars, awarded three months after it opened in July 2025. Gault&Millau gives it 18 points and it is tenth in the 2026 Lekker list. The chef is Soenil Bahadoer.

Can you stay overnight at Restaurant GEM.?

Yes, in two suites on the grounds of Kasteel Gemert: the Donjon Tower Suite in the castle and the Garden Suite in the castle garden. Breakfast is prepared by the GEM. team. In 2027 Kasteel Gemert opens as a five-star hotel with seventy rooms.

What does Dine & Stay at Restaurant GEM. cost?

For two, we count on about 950 euros for dinner, one night and breakfast at Restaurant GEM. (November 2026). That is the Dine & Stay package with six courses, a suite, champagne or minibar and breakfast, on Wednesdays and Thursdays. Drinks with dinner are extra.

Does Restaurant GEM. have a plant-based menu?

Yes. Alongside the six-course GEM. menu, Restaurant GEM. offers a fully plant-based six-course menu, with dishes such as saag paneer with samosa and potato masala. There is also an alcohol-free drinks pairing.

When is Restaurant GEM. open?

Restaurant GEM. at Kasteel Gemert is open Wednesday to Sunday. The Dine & Stay package with an overnight stay can only be booked on Wednesday and Thursday evenings.

Where is Restaurant GEM.?

Restaurant GEM. is at Kasteellaan 1 in Gemert, in the medieval gatehouse of Kasteel Gemert, in the Brabant Peel. Eindhoven is about half an hour by car. By train, travel to Helmond or Eindhoven and take a bus or taxi.
