Dine and stay the night: where we eat and sleep

Dinner and a bed under one roof, all over the Netherlands.

We love going out for dinner, and we like to stay the night afterwards so nobody has to drive. This book holds 41 addresses in twelve provinces where you dine and sleep under one roof, with what it costs for two.

In short

What is a restaurant with rooms?

A restaurant where you can stay the night after dinner, at the same address. This book only lists places where you dine and sleep under one roof.

Which address is number one?

Piloersemaborg in Den Ham, followed by Van Oys Maastricht Retreat and De Nederlanden.
