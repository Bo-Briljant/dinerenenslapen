Dine and stay in Limburg

Where we dine and sleep in Limburg

Hills, vineyards and castles built on marl. Limburg is the southernmost chapter and one of the richest: two-star kitchens, terraced castles, monasteries and castle hotels to stay in after dinner.

In Limburg we keep it to four addresses. There are more, but we only write down the places we would go back to. If we had to pick one, it would be Van Oys Maastricht Retreat. If you are coming for a star, try Van Oys Maastricht Retreat and Château Neercanne. The next place is still empty: we are still testing.

Questions about dining and staying in Limburg

Where can you dine and stay the night in Limburg?

Our book lists four addresses in Limburg: Van Oys Maastricht Retreat in Eijsden, Château Neercanne in Maastricht, Château St. Gerlach in Valkenburg aan de Geul and Kasteeltje Hattem in Roermond.

Where would you go yourselves in Limburg?

Van Oys Maastricht Retreat in Eijsden. Braeken used to cook at Château Wittem and has built something special again in a converted barn on this estate. After dinner, your room is on the same estate by the Maas. If it is full, we ring Château Neercanne.

What does dinner with an overnight stay cost in Limburg?

For two, count on € 340 at Kasteeltje Hattem up to € 670 at Château Neercanne, for dinner, one night and breakfast. Amounts come from their own booking systems, for a weeknight in November 2026.

Can you stay the night at a Michelin-starred restaurant in Limburg?

Yes: at Van Oys Maastricht Retreat in Eijsden and Château Neercanne in Maastricht. After dessert you just walk upstairs.
