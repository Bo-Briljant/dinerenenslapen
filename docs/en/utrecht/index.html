Where we dine and stay the night in Utrecht

Country houses along the Vecht, estates on the Utrecht Hill Ridge and a monastery in the heart of the city. In Utrecht a starred dinner with a bed upstairs is never far from home.

For now, the book has three addresses in Utrecht. When we find more that belong here, we will add them. If we had to choose just one, it would be De Nederlanden. If you are after a Michelin star, you will find one there, and also at Parc Broekhuizen and Grand Hotel Karel V. The next place is still empty: we are keeping it for the address we are about to try.

Dining and staying in Utrecht: where and when

For much of the Netherlands, Utrecht is the province where a starred dinner with an overnight stay is closest to home. All three addresses hold a Michelin star. De Nederlanden in Vreeland lies on the Vecht, among the country estates, Parc Broekhuizen in Leersum is on an estate on the Utrechtse Heuvelrug, and Grand Hotel Karel V occupies a former monastery in the middle of the city of Utrecht. For two you pay about 380 to 520 euros here.

The choice is mainly between city and countryside. At Karel V you walk along the canals to the Dom tower after dinner, and you can easily get there by train. Vreeland and Leersum are addresses for a weekend in the country, with walking and cycling along the Vecht or across the Heuvelrug, which is at its finest in autumn. Because the province is so central, it is also a good choice for a surprise on a weeknight: little travel time, and the next morning you are home again quickly.

Frequently asked questions about dining and staying in Utrecht

Where can you dine and stay the night in Utrecht?

Our book has three addresses in Utrecht: De Nederlanden in Vreeland, Parc Broekhuizen in Leersum and Grand Hotel Karel V in Utrecht.

Where do you most like to go in Utrecht?

De Nederlanden in Vreeland. Wilco Berends has cooked at De Nederlanden since 2006 and has owned this house on the Vecht since 2012, and his oyster tasting is its calling card. Above the restaurant are eleven calm rooms with Dutch design and Nilson beds. If it is fully booked, we ring Parc Broekhuizen.

What does dinner with an overnight stay cost in Utrecht?

For dinner, one night and breakfast, two people pay from € 380 at De Nederlanden to € 520 at Grand Hotel Karel V. We take the amounts from each address's own booking system, for a weeknight in November 2026.

Can you stay the night at a Michelin-starred restaurant in Utrecht?

Yes: at De Nederlanden in Vreeland, Parc Broekhuizen in Leersum and Grand Hotel Karel V in Utrecht. After dessert, bed is just up the stairs.

Which is the smallest and which the largest address in Utrecht?

The smallest is De Nederlanden, with 11 rooms. The largest is Grand Hotel Karel V, with 121 rooms and suites. The smaller the house, the earlier you need to book.
