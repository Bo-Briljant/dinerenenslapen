Dine and stay in Flevoland

Where we dine and sleep in Flevoland

The youngest province, reclaimed from the sea. The choice is small and unpretentious: a tower room above the polder, fresh fish on Urk and a family hotel by the woods. This selection will grow with the province.

In Flevoland we keep it to two addresses. There are more, but we only write down the places we would go back to. If we had to pick one, it would be 't Voorhuys. The next place is still empty: we are still testing.

Questions about dining and staying in Flevoland

Where can you dine and stay the night in Flevoland?

Our book lists two addresses in Flevoland: 't Voorhuys in Emmeloord and Hotel Van Saaze in Kraggenburg.

Where would you go yourselves in Flevoland?

't Voorhuys in Emmeloord. Not a fine dining address, but you can sleep inside the symbol of the Noordoostpolder. We go for the cheerful buzz downstairs and the unusual bed in the tower. If it is full, we ring Hotel Van Saaze.

What does dinner with an overnight stay cost in Flevoland?

For two, count on € 200 at Hotel Van Saaze up to € 225 at 't Voorhuys, for dinner, one night and breakfast. Amounts come from their own booking systems, for a weeknight in November 2026.

Can you stay the night at a Michelin-starred restaurant in Flevoland?

Not at a starred address yet. But at places we like just as much.
