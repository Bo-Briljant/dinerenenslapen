Top 10 restaurants with rooms in the Netherlands

Ten places where the evening at the table and the night upstairs are worth the trip together

Our own list, in order of our three scores: food, stay and price. 9 of the ten hold a Michelin star, they are spread over 7 provinces, and the most affordable is De Nederlanden, approx. € 380 for two.

1

Manor farm with restaurant and rooms

Piloersemaborg

9.3our score

Den Ham · Groningen · approx. € 410 for two

Gault&Millau 14/20Lekker500 nr. 71

A box bed after six courses. We ask for nothing more.

Why we go The last borg farmhouse in Groningen, run by a chef who moved his entire restaurant here. If we're honest, the box bed is what draws us most.

Read our review

2

Five-star castle estate hotel with two-star restaurant

Van Oys Maastricht Retreat

9.3our score

Eijsden · Limburg · approx. € 550 for two

2 Michelin starsGault&Millau 17/20Leading Hotels of the World

A chestnut tree on the ceiling and Guido Braeken in the kitchen.

Why we go Braeken used to cook at Château Wittem and has built something special again in a converted barn on this estate. After dinner, your room is on the same estate by the Maas.

Read our review

3

Starred restaurant with boutique hotel on the river Vecht

De Nederlanden

9.0our score

Vreeland · Utrecht · approx. € 380 for two

One Michelin starGault&Millau 17/20Les Patrons CuisiniersQuality Lodgings

Oysters by the Vecht, then simply up to bed.

Why we go Wilco and Caroline Berends in a building from 1680, with the lift bridge in view. We come for the French cooking, oysters above all, and stay for the calm rooms full of Dutch design.

Read our review

4

Terraced castle with starred restaurant and suites

Château Neercanne

9.0our score

Maastricht · Limburg · approx. € 670 for two

One Michelin starBib GourmandGault&Millau 15.5/20

A castle set into the hillside, with the wine kept in the caves.

Why we go Robert Levels cooks here looking out over the baroque gardens and the Jeker valley. And since 2024 you no longer have to go home after dinner.

Read our review

5

Grand hotel with two-star restaurant

De L'Europe Amsterdam

8.9our score

Amsterdam · North Holland · approx. € 1,055 for two

2 Michelin starsGault&Millau 18/20Leading Hotels of the World

A grand hotel on the Amstel whose kitchen skips dairy.

Why we go On the Amstel since 1896 and owned by the Heineken family for decades. We go for Flore, where Bas van Kranen cooks to the rhythm of the Dutch micro-seasons.

Read our review

6

Boutique hotel with starred restaurant on a country estate

Central Park Voorburg

8.9our score

Voorburg · South Holland · approx. € 500 for two

One Michelin starGault&Millau 15.5/20Relais & Châteaux

A wine list worth staying for, so we stay.

Why we go A country house from 1751 in a park by Zocher, where kitchen, cellar and bed are close together. We come here so we needn't watch the clock at dinner.

Read our review

7

Boutique hotel in a historic city palace with Michelin-starred restaurant

Prinsenhof & Restaurant Nassau

8.9our score

Groningen · Groningen · approx. € 400 for two

One Michelin starGault&Millau 15/20

Dinner in a prince's old bedchambers. You couldn't make it up.

Why we go A city palace with roots in the fifteenth century, right beside the Prinsentuin. It's where we go when we want a night in town that doesn't end with hunting for a taxi.

Read our review

8

Two-star restaurant with suites

De Lindenhof

8.9our score

Giethoorn · Overijssel · approx. € 525 for two

2 Michelin starsGault&Millau 18/20Les Patrons Cuisiniers

Out on the water in the chef's boat first, then dinner.

Why we go A thatched farmhouse in Giethoorn where Martin Kruithof has been cooking at the top level since the nineties. What draws us is that steady hand, and a night with the kitchen garden next door.

Read our review

9

Country estate hotel with Michelin-starred restaurant and bistro

Landgoed Lauswolt

8.8our score

Beetsterzwaag · Friesland · approx. € 435 for two

One Michelin starBib GourmandGault&Millau 16.5/20Lekker500 nr. 43

An estate where you needn't go past the gate all weekend.

Why we go A stately country house near Beetsterzwaag with two serious kitchens on one estate. This is what we choose when we'd rather not choose between a big night and an easy one.

Read our review

10

Country house hotel with starred restaurant and spa

De Bloemenbeek

8.8our score

De Lutte · Overijssel · approx. € 420 for two

One Michelin starGault&Millau 16/20SVH MeesterkokAlliance Gastronomique

Two chefs with over half a century in this kitchen between them.

Why we go A country house hotel near De Lutte with French-Mediterranean cooking, its own spa and herbs from the garden and greenhouse. We go for a long weekend with dinner as the high point.
