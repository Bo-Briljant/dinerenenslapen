Dine and stay in Drenthe

Where we dine and sleep in Drenthe

Heathland, dolmens and villages around a green. Drenthe is the province of manor houses and country hotels, where the kitchen draws on its own garden and woods and the evening ends by the fire.

In Drenthe we keep it to three addresses. There are more, but we only write down the places we would go back to. If we had to pick one, it would be Landhuishotel & Restaurant Rikus. The next place is still empty: we are still testing.

Named after the last farmer on the land. A lovely start.

Questions about dining and staying in Drenthe

Where can you dine and stay the night in Drenthe?

Our book lists three addresses in Drenthe: Landhuishotel & Restaurant Rikus in Eext, Château De Havixhorst in De Schiphorst and POSTEN Nordic Cuisine in Frederiksoord.

Where would you go yourselves in Drenthe?

Landhuishotel & Restaurant Rikus in Eext. A Drenthe farmhouse from 1922 surrounded by its own vegetable garden, woodland and open fields. We go for the vegetables from the yard and a walk across the land before dinner. If it is full, we ring Château De Havixhorst.

What does dinner with an overnight stay cost in Drenthe?

For two, count on € 295 at POSTEN Nordic Cuisine up to € 330 at Château De Havixhorst, for dinner, one night and breakfast. Amounts come from their own booking systems, for a weeknight in November 2026.

Can you stay the night at a Michelin-starred restaurant in Drenthe?

Not at a starred address yet. But at places we like just as much.
